# Subagent result: stack

## Key Facts
- **Network:** Monad mainnet launched **24 Nov 2025** and, through **30 Aug 2026**, reported 100% uptime; 99.903% proposal success and 99.999% finalization success. Mainnet is live on chain **143**, native token **MON**. Testnet remains live on chain **10143**; it was reset from genesis 16 Dec 2025 and still exposes `https://faucet.monad.xyz`. (https://www.monad.xyz/blog/monad-network-uptime; https://docs.monad.xyz/ai/current-facts; as of 12 Sep 2026)
- **Canonical performance:** **300 ms blocks**, **300 ms speculative finality**, **600 ms full finality**, **10,000+ typical-transfer TPS design capacity**, **30M per-transaction gas cap**. Current Facts reports an observed **150M block gas limit / 500M gas/s**; another live developer-summary page says **200M / 500M gas/s**, so query the current chain before sizing workloads. (https://docs.monad.xyz/ai/current-facts; https://docs.monad.xyz/developer-essentials/summary; as of 12 Sep 2026)
- **Funding:** Testnet MON remains available from the official faucet. For mainnet, MON is exchange-listed (official July update names Coinbase, Revolut and Interactive Brokers); buy/withdraw a small amount or acquire it through a supported onramp/bridge route. Stablecoins cannot pay native gas. No official “mainnet faucet” was found. (https://docs.monad.xyz/developer-essentials/testnet; https://www.monad.xyz/blog/monad-economy-eight-months-in; as of 12 Sep 2026)
- **Public RPC:** `rpc.monad.xyz`/WSS (QuickNode, 25 rps, batch 100), `rpc1.monad.xyz`/WSS (Alchemy, 15 rps, batch 100; debug/trace disabled), `rpc2.monad.xyz`/WSS (Goldsky Edge, 300/10s, batch 10), `rpc3.monad.xyz`/WSS (Ankr, 300/10s, batch 10), and `rpc-mainnet.monadinfra.com`/WSS (Foundation, 20 rps, batch 1). Testnet canonical endpoint is `testnet-rpc.monad.xyz` plus WSS. (https://docs.monad.xyz/developer-essentials/network-information; as of 12 Sep 2026)
- **RPC constraints:** `eth_getLogs` spans are only 100 blocks on QuickNode/Foundation and 1,000 on Alchemy/Ankr (Alchemy also caps 10,000 logs). `eth_call`/estimate caps are 200M on QuickNode/Alchemy/Foundation and 1B on Ankr. Monad supports standard and commitment-aware `monadNewHeads`/`monadLogs` subscriptions, but not pending-tx or syncing subscriptions. Full nodes do **not** guarantee arbitrary historic state; Foundation and Goldsky public endpoints advertise historical state lookups, while Envio advertises read-only historical access up to 10,000 blocks. (https://docs.monad.xyz/reference/json-rpc/overview; https://docs.monad.xyz/tooling-and-infra/rpc-providers; as of 12 Sep 2026)
- **RPC vendors:** Official live support includes QuickNode, Alchemy, Chainstack, Dwellir, Spectrum, Envio, Ankr, Blockdaemon, BlockPI, dRPC, GetBlock, OnFinality, Tatum, thirdweb, Triton One and Validation Cloud. Chainstack explicitly offers free signup; Alchemy a free tier. The official docs do **not** currently list Crouton Digital or provider-specific Metropolis credits. (https://docs.monad.xyz/tooling-and-infra/rpc-providers; as of 12 Sep 2026)
- **Explorers/verification:** Mainnet: **MonadVision** (BlockVision) and **Monadscan** (Etherscan); testnet variants exist. MonadVision verifier is Sourcify-compatible at `sourcify-api-monad.blockvision.org`; Monadscan uses Etherscan APIs. JiffyScan covers UserOps; Tenderly and Phalcon provide deep traces. Socialscan is not in the current official list. (https://docs.monad.xyz/tooling-and-infra/block-explorers; as of 12 Sep 2026)
- **Wallets:** MetaMask, Rabby, Backpack and OKX are officially supported. Add a custom EVM network with RPC `https://rpc.monad.xyz`, chain 143, symbol MON, explorer `https://monadvision.com`; many wallets autodetect it. **Phantom is no longer supported—it removed Monad on 26 Aug 2026**, so do not target it for Metropolis. (https://docs.monad.xyz/tooling-and-infra/wallets/software-wallets; https://help.phantom.com/hc/en-us/articles/38674346854931-Ending-support-for-Monad; as of 12 Sep 2026)
- **Core tooling:** Foundry requires **v1.8.0+** and `network = "monad"`/`--network monad`; Hardhat’s official example uses Solidity **0.8.28** with `evmVersion: "prague"`. Current docs say all **Fusaka** opcodes/precompiles are supported, but type-3/EIP-4844 blob transactions remain unsupported. viem exports `monad` and `monadTestnet`; wagmi consumes these viem chain definitions. (https://docs.monad.xyz/tooling-and-infra/toolkits/foundry; https://docs.monad.xyz/tooling-and-infra/toolkits/hardhat; https://github.com/wevm/viem/blob/main/src/chains/definitions/monad.ts; as of 12 Sep 2026)
- **Precompiles/passkeys:** Ethereum precompiles `0x01–0x11` plus P256 at **`0x0100`**, staking at `0x1000`, reserve-balance at `0x1001`. P256 now follows **EIP-7951**, which supersedes RIP-7212 but keeps the same `0x100` address/interface; it costs 6,900 gas and enables WebAuthn/passkey verification. (https://docs.monad.xyz/developer-essentials/precompiles; as of 12 Sep 2026)
- **EIP-7702/AA:** Type `0x04` delegation is live. ERC-4337 EntryPoints v0.6/v0.7/v0.8 are deployed. Officially listed AA stacks are **Biconomy, MetaMask Smart Accounts Kit, Pimlico and ZeroDev**; Alchemy Account Kit separately provides Gas Manager and Bundler APIs. MetaMask supports embedded 4337/7702 accounts, WebAuthn, multisig and ERC-7710 delegation. (https://docs.monad.xyz/developer-essentials/eip-7702; https://docs.monad.xyz/tooling-and-infra/wallet-infra/smart-accounts; https://docs.monad.xyz/tooling-and-infra/wallet-infra/embedded-wallets; as of 12 Sep 2026)
- **Embedded wallets:** Privy, Dynamic, Turnkey, Mera, Alchemy, MetaMask Embedded Wallets and others are marked supported. Mera is open-source, self-custodial passkey-derived BIP-44 EOAs with no backend/bundler; Turnkey offers TEE-backed policies/sessions; Privy/Dynamic offer passkey/social/email/SMS onboarding. Privy subsidizes testnet use; Turnkey is free on testnet. (https://docs.monad.xyz/tooling-and-infra/wallet-infra/embedded-wallets; as of 12 Sep 2026)
- **Indexing/data:** Envio HyperIndex supports chain 143 with HyperSync `https://143.hypersync.xyz` and read-only HyperRPC `https://143.rpc.hypersync.xyz`. The Graph, Goldsky, Envio, SQD, SubQuery and StreamingFast are supported; Goldsky supplies Subgraphs, Mirror and Turbo. Dune has raw blocks/transactions/logs/traces plus decoded contracts/events. Zerion API supports wallet balances, transactions, PNL and webhooks. Ponder, Nansen-on-Monad API support and a distinct enhanced Alchemy indexing API were not confirmed in current official pages. (https://docs.envio.dev/docs/HyperIndex/monad; https://docs.monad.xyz/tooling-and-infra/indexers/indexing-frameworks; https://docs.monad.xyz/tooling-and-infra/indexers/common-data; https://docs.dune.com/data-catalog/evm/monad/overview; as of 12 Sep 2026)
- **Oracles/offchain:** Mainnet officially marks **Chainlink Price Feeds + Data Streams, Pyth, RedStone, Stork, Switchboard, Chronicle and Supra** live. Pyth is pull-based with ~400 ms publisher updates; Switchboard provides pull feeds, aggregation, custom/API-gated feeds and VRF. Chainlink **CCIP** is live (router `0x33566fE5976AAa420F3d5C64996641Fc3858CaDB`) with many lanes. CRE is a general Chainlink runtime, but no Monad-specific CRE deployment/support statement was verified. (https://docs.monad.xyz/tooling-and-infra/oracles; https://docs.chain.link/ccip/directory/mainnet/chain/monad-mainnet; as of 12 Sep 2026)
- **Bridges/liquidity:** Officially live mainnet integrations include **Wormhole/Portal, LayerZero, deBridge, Across, Chainlink CCIP and Circle CCTP**, plus many aggregators/liquidity layers. Aurora/NEAR Intents is not listed under that name in current Monad docs. (https://docs.monad.xyz/tooling-and-infra/cross-chain; as of 12 Sep 2026)
- **Stablecoins:** Circle confirms **native USDC** on Monad; CCTP provides native burn/mint transfers. **AUSD** is live (Agora reported 10,000 Monad holders on 21 Jul 2026). **USDT0** is live at `0xe7cd86e13AC4309349F30B3435a9d337750fC82D`, using LayerZero and 3-of-3 attestation. Official Monad’s July report names USDC, USDT, EURW and AUSD; **USDtb was not verified**. (https://www.circle.com/multi-chain-usdc/monad; https://www.agora.finance/blog; https://usdt0.to/ecosystem/monad; as of 12 Sep 2026)
- **Monad gotchas:** transactions are charged against **gas limit, not gas used**; async submission may initially accept nonce-gap/insufficient-balance transactions; no global mempool or pending-tx lookup; `latest` is speculative/proposed, `safe` voted, `finalized` irreversible, and financially sensitive offchain systems should wait for **Verified (T+5)** state-root finality. EIP-7702 accounts cannot be reduced below the 10 MON reserve and delegated code cannot use CREATE/CREATE2. Max runtime code is 128 KB, initcode 256 KB; memory is linear-priced but capped at 8 MB; storage warms in 128-slot pages. (https://docs.monad.xyz/developer-essentials/differences; https://docs.monad.xyz/reference/json-rpc/overview; as of 12 Sep 2026)
- **High-frequency practice:** batch/concurrent RPC calls, use Multicall3 where appropriate, hardcode static gas, index events instead of repeatedly scanning logs, track nonces locally and submit independent transactions concurrently. Parallel execution preserves serial semantics, so contract correctness needs no special rewrite. (https://docs.monad.xyz/developer-essentials/best-practices; as of 12 Sep 2026)
- **AI/agents:** Monad has an OAuth remote MCP endpoint (`https://app.monad.com/mcp`), x402 facilitator supporting USDC with sponsored settlement, canonical x402 proxy deployments, and ERC-8004 identity/reputation registries at `0x8004A169FB4a3325136EB29fA0ceB6D2e539a432` / `0x8004BAa17C55a88189AE136b182e5fdA19dE9b63`; Validation Registry remains “coming soon.” agent0 has TS/Python SDKs. (https://app.monad.com/docs/mcp/index; https://docs.monad.xyz/tooling-and-infra/agentic-payments; https://docs.monad.xyz/guides/erc-8004; as of 12 Sep 2026)

- Live docs conflict on observed block gas limit: **150M** Current Facts versus **200M** Developer Summary; use `eth_getBlockByNumber` immediately before launch/load tests.
- `network-information` showed v0.15.2 while canonical Current Facts showed mainnet v0.16.1/testnet v0.16.2; likely stale page rendering. Prefer Current Facts/network JSON/release log.
- Hardhat still recommends Prague while the deployment summary says Fusaka supported. Compile conservatively and test using Monad-enabled Foundry.
- No primary evidence found for Crouton Digital Metropolis credits, archive tiers/rate limits for every commercial vendor, Aurora/NEAR Intents, Ponder, Nansen API, or USDtb.
- `viem`’s testnet definition says 400 ms while current canonical Monad facts say 300 ms; chain metadata is not a timing guarantee.
- No current official `scaffold-monad` or `create-monad-app` recommendation surfaced; verified official templates are `monad-developers/foundry-monad` and a miniapp template. Treat similarly named community repos as noncanonical.

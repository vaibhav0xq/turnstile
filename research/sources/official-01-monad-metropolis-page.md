Why Metropolis
The onchain economy needs a metropolis. What would you build? Metropolis is a six-week sprint to build what’s next on the onchain frontier.
What you get
$250,000+
In prizes, across four tracks, a grand champion and sponsor bounties.
What to build
Four tracks
Curated tracks to build at the frontier of onchain innovation.

Onchain Finance & Trading
Fast settlement makes new financial instruments and fully onchain markets more practical.

Teams who have shipped a trading, lending, or market-making product before.
Fully onchain order books that do not need an offchain matching engine
Perpetuals with funding that updates every block
Undercollateralised lending priced on onchain credit history

FAQ
Questions
Who can participate?
Anyone building a product on Monad. Teams and solo builders, crypto-native or not. You do not need prior onchain experience and you do not need to have raised.
Are there country restrictions?
Metropolis is online and open globally. Any eligibility restrictions are listed in the official rules on the application platform.
Can I participate solo?
Yes. Teams and solo builders are both welcome.
Can I submit an existing project?
Yes, if the work you submit is new. Bring an existing team and existing context, but what you show on 13 Oct should have been built during the six weeks.
Does my project need to be open source?
Open sourcing is encouraged but not required. Judges need to be able to verify what you built during the six weeks.
How do I choose a track?
Pick the direction closest to what you want to build — each track lists best-fit teams and example ideas above. You choose one when you apply.
Is the hackathon online only?
The build itself runs online. Through the build window we also host one-day activations in cities where Monad teams already are.
What do I submit?
A working product with a public project profile: a demo, a short write-up, and a link to the code.
How does judging work?
Projects are judged per track after the 13 Oct deadline, from 14 to 27 Oct, with winners announced on 3 Nov.
What do winners receive?
A share of the $250,000 prize pool — $30,000 per track and an overall winner — plus sponsor bounties awarded on top.
What happens after the hackathon?
Your project profile stays live, and any team can carry it into DeltaV, where 800+ Monad startups already get credits, intros, and office hours.
How can my company get involved?
Sponsor a bounty, mentor, or judge — reach out through Partner with us.

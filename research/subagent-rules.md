# Subagent result: rules

## Key Facts
- **Core rules (as of 2026-09-12):** Global; solo builders and teams accepted; no prior onchain experience/fundraising required. Existing projects are allowed only where the submitted work is new and built during 1 Sep–13 Oct. Open source is encouraged, not mandatory, but judges must be able to verify the six-week work. Applicants choose a track when applying; the wording does **not** expressly say whether multiple projects/tracks are prohibited. (https://monad.xyz/developers/hackathons/metropolis)
- **Submission:** Official wording is “a working product with a public project profile: a demo, a short write-up, and a link to the code.” It does **not** say “demo video”; therefore a demo is required, but a video requirement is unverified. (https://monad.xyz/developers/hackathons/metropolis)
- **Network:** The logged-out application page is indexed as “Network · Monad Chain ID · 143,” indicating mainnet context. Official rules only say build a product “on Monad”; no source found explicitly requires mainnet deployment, permits testnet 10143, or defines deployment minimums. (https://hackathon.monad.xyz/)
- **Unknown until authenticated official rules are obtained:** age/minor rules, excluded countries/sanctions, maximum team size, prize KYC/tax requirements, ownership/IP terms, and formal one-track/multiple-submission policy. The public FAQ explicitly defers country restrictions to “official rules on the application platform.” (https://monad.xyz/developers/hackathons/metropolis)
- **No mandatory milestone check-ins found:** the official page promises mentorship throughout. Existing-project work must be verifiably new. Do not confuse this with evm/accathon’s 2025 ideation check-in or grant milestones. (official page above; https://hackathon.monad.xyz/ archive)
- **Registration cutoff:** Registration opened 1 Sep and the platform was indexed “Intake · Open” on 12 Sep. No official or press source found a 13 Sep cutoff despite exact-phrase searches. Official dates are submission 13 Oct, judging 14–27 Oct, winners 3 Nov. (https://hackathon.monad.xyz/; https://monad.xyz/developers/hackathons/metropolis)
- **Rise In discrepancy:** Its header shows Aug 31/Oct 12/Nov 2, while its own timeline shows Sep 1/Oct 13/Nov 3. This consistent one-day shift is a timezone-rendering artifact, not evidence of an earlier deadline. Treat Rise In as unofficial and follow Monad’s dates. (https://www.risein.com/monad/monad-metropolis-hackathon)
- **News check:** Gate’s 2 Sep report, attributed to Foresight News, also says submission closes 13 Oct; it gives no separate registration cutoff. CoinMarketCap’s currently fetched “latest updates” page contained no retrievable Metropolis item. (https://www.gate.com/en-us/news/detail/monad-launches-metropolis-global-hackathon-with-250k-prize-pool-deadline-17859865; https://coinmarketcap.com/cmc-ai/monad/latest-updates)
- **Hacker houses:** Luma registrations for NYC, Hangzhou, SF, Shenzhen and London require host approval; no page states an application deadline. Hangzhou and Shenzhen expressly say offline and online participation are independent. London says its meetup is not a substitute for registering at hackathon.monad.xyz. NYC/SF accept Metropolis participants or local builders. (https://luma.com/monad-metropolis and city pages)
- **Local dates/status:** Official index gives NYC 18 Sep; Hangzhou is 19 Sep; official Metropolis page gives SF 25 Sep, London 25 Sep, Shenzhen 26 Sep and Singapore 6 Oct. Buenos Aires appears on the official Luma calendar under Crecimiento + Monad Foundation, but the fetched calendar exposed neither date nor standalone URL. Singapore is part of “Open”; GA and VIP require approval and Luma asks applicants to allow two weeks for review, but gives no cutoff. (official/Luma sources above; https://luma.com/open-2026)
- **Metropolis judging:** Only “judged per track” during 14–27 Oct is published. No rubric, weights, pitch round, judge scoring process, or tie-breaker was found. (https://monad.xyz/developers/hackathons/metropolis)
- **Best precedent—Raingentic Commerce (Aug 2026):** 35 submissions; private scoring on four published criteria—**idea, technical execution, presentation/documentation, wow factor**—then five finalists pitched live. Winners were working, sharply scoped agentic-commerce products using real Rain/Monad primitives. This is recent Monad-cohosted precedent, not a Metropolis rubric. (https://www.blog.encode.club/raingentic-commerce-hackathon-nyc-2026-8cbea2addbc9)
- **Blitz precedent (Mar 2026 description):** One-day IRL format with architecture/tool workshops, sprint, testnet prototype deployment and demos; winners selected by live audience vote. About 1,700 unique developers had participated as of publication. (https://www.monad.xyz/blog/home-for-builders)
- **Moltiverse precedent (Feb 2026):** Own site, not a Devfolio/DoraHacks flow; “ship proof (not promises),” build publicly, demo/iterate/publish frequent updates, rolling judging, up to 16 winners. One liquidity award was objectively based on final nad.fun market cap. Official X reported 16 winners from 400 submissions. (https://moltiverse.dev/)
- **evm/accathon precedent (Feb–Mar 2025, stale >12 months):** Selective application by 11 Feb; information session and ideation check-in before a two-day Denver event plus two online weeks. No public rubric or video rule found. Winners entered a capped-$10k/team NYC Builder Residency; participants could receive up to $30k milestone-based grants. (https://www.monad.xyz/blog/the-first-monad-hackathon-evm-accathon)
- **Monad Madness precedent (2024, stale):** Venture-ready, working MVP/demo, fundraising intent and in-person investor pitch. Application pitch video was strongly encouraged, not required; no published weights found. It is a pitch competition, not a coding-hackathon submission template. (https://madness.monad.xyz/)
- **Submission platform precedent:** Current Metropolis uses a custom Monad application flow with GitHub/Google/Discord login. No evidence found that Metropolis, evm/accathon, Blitz or Moltiverse uses Devfolio or DoraHacks. Devfolio’s generic rubric must not be attributed to Monad. (https://hackathon.monad.xyz/)
- **DeltaV (as of 2026-09-12):** Official indexed copy describes a multi-chain founder/startup graph where Monad is the “gravitational center,” with programs, contributors and investors. It currently shows inconsistent figures—“1K Startups” headline versus “659 Startups” map count—so the circulated “800+” should be treated as a moving/rounded claim, not a precise current total. (https://deltav.monad.xyz/)
- **Residency meaning:** Metropolis guarantees only that top teams “get invited to a residency program alongside other top teams on Monad”; it does not publish which cohort, selection count, location, duration, funding, or whether invitation equals admission. The separate 2025 Founder Residency page describes a stale cohort (10 projects, three weeks, Malaysia, lodging/meals/workspace/program covered but not travel); do not assume those terms apply in 2026. (official Metropolis page; https://residency.monad.xyz/)
- **Official channels:** Community Discord https://discord.gg/monad; developer Discord https://discord.gg/monaddev; Telegram announcements https://t.me/monad_xyz; developer announcements https://t.me/monad_devs; X https://x.com/monad and https://x.com/monad_dev; developer portal https://developers.monad.xyz; weekly newsletter https://news.monad.xyz. Metropolis local updates are centralized at https://luma.com/monad-metropolis; official hackathon/event indexes also list workshops. No single detailed online Metropolis workshop calendar was found. (https://docs.monad.xyz/official-links)

## Notable Claims Requiring Cross-Reference
- “Demo required” is official; “demo **video** required” is not supported publicly.
- Chain ID 143 appears on the platform landing metadata, but an explicit mainnet-deployment rule is absent.
- London Luma copy says “$30,000 overall winner,” conflicting with the official Metropolis page’s $25,000 Grand Champion; use the official page.
- Official page says seven cities, but fetched body visibly listed only five; Luma adds NYC and Buenos Aires, while Singapore appears through Open.
- No evidence supports the 13 Sep registration rumor. Absence of public evidence is not a substitute for authenticated platform rules.
- DeltaV startup counts are internally inconsistent and dynamic.

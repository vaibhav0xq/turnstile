# research

Phase 1 and 2 of the hackathon work (September 2026), kept in-repo so decisions stay traceable. These files
are dated records and are not updated. Where they differ from the root `README.md`, the README is current:
the selected bounties are Best Mera-Powered UX on Monad, Mera: One Passkey, Many Keys, Best Use of Envio and
Best Projects using Alchemy. Privy and the Community Team Project were considered here and dropped.

- `monad-metropolis-report.md`: hackathon, tracks, bounties, stack facts, competitor scan (Phase 1).
- `turnstile-build-plan.md`: the solo build plan: scope, design (§8, now `packages/identity/SPEC.md`), milestones.
- `bounty-report.md`: bounty-by-bounty fit and the target list as it stood on 12 Sep 2026.
- `subagent-*.md`: synthesised notes from the parallel research passes (rules, stack, ecosystem, UX docs, design references).
- `notes.md`: running log with dates.
- `sources.json`: every URL consulted, with fetch dates.
- `sources/official-*`: captures of the official hackathon pages (the only third-party captures kept; the ~6 MB of vendor-docs and design-site scrapes referenced in `sources.json` are not committed).
